KSU Soccer League
- 4 Teams
- Each Team faces the other teams twice (Home and Away)
- Every week we have two games.
- In one season, we have 6 weeks with 12 games:
	W1: 1v2, 3v4
	W2: 1v3, 2v4
	W3: 1v4, 2v3
	W4: 2v1, 4v3
	W5: 3v1, 4v2
	W6: 4v1, 3v2
- A wins gives a team 3 pts, draws gives both teams 1 pts, loses 0 pts.

Requirements:
Build a system that the user is able to input the outcome of each game.

The system should track a table of each team with their respective points.

At the end of each week, the system should sort and print the table. 
The sorting should be from the team with the highest point to the lowest point.
- Do not bother with conflicts such as teams having the same point.
